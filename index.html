How to Play Spades

Teams & Objective

  • Partnerships: You & Helen play against Frank & Alice.
  • Winning: First team to reach 500 points at the end of a hand wins. If both teams tie at 500+, one tiebreaker hand is played.

Bidding & Gameplay

  • Bidding: Each player is dealt 13 cards and bids how many tricks they expect to win (0/Nil to 13). Partners' non-Nil bids combine into one team contract.
  • Following Suit: You must play a card of the led suit if you hold one. If void, you may play any card, including a Spade (trump).
  • Winning a Trick: The highest Spade wins; otherwise, the highest card of the led suit wins. The trick winner leads the next trick.

Scoring

  • Make Team Bid: +10 points per trick bid.
  • Overtricks ("Bags"): +1 point and +1 bag per extra trick. Reaching 10 bags incurs a -100 point penalty.
  • Missed Bid ("Set"): -10 points per trick bid.
  • Nil (0 Bid): +100 points if you take 0 tricks; -100 points if you take 1 or more (scored separately from your partner's bid).
